Activities at Pacific Trails

Hiking

Pacific Trails Resort has five miles of hiking trails and is adjacent to a state park. Go hiking and enjoy the beautiful scenery.

Kayaking

Ocean kayaks are available for guest use. Experience the Pacific Ocean in a whole new way.

Bird Watching

The Pacific Coast offers many opportunities to observe native birds in their natural environment.
