The Yurts at Pacific Trails

What is a yurt?
Our luxury yurts are permanent structures four feet off the ground. Each yurt has canvas walls, a wooden floor, and a roof dome.
How are the yurts furnished?
Each yurt is furnished with a queen-size bed, gas fireplace, and a private deck.
What should I bring?
Bring a sense of adventure and some warm clothing. All other amenities are provided.
